# Payment failure and retry

If a payment fails but the account is debited, wait 30 minutes and check the order page before retrying.
An unsuccessful debit is normally reversed by the payment provider within 3 to 5 business days.
Retry only when the order page confirms payment failure, and use the same order's payment link to avoid a duplicate purchase.
If the debit remains after 5 business days, submit the transaction reference through the secure support form without sharing a card number or OTP.
