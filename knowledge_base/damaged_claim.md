# Damaged item claim process

Report a damaged, leaking, or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery using the order's Help section.
Attach clear photographs of the item, shipping label, and outer packaging, and retain the packaging until the claim is resolved.
The claims team reviews the evidence within 2 business days and confirms an eligible replacement or refund after verification.
Do not use a damaged electrical device or a leaking cosmetic while waiting for the review.
