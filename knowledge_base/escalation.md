# Customer support escalation matrix

Level 1 support handles routine policy and order-status questions, with an acknowledgement target of 4 business hours.
Level 2 logistics handles stalled deliveries and failed pickups, while Level 2 payments handles overdue refunds and unreversed debits, with a response target of 1 business day.
Level 3 grievance review handles unresolved Level 2 cases after 3 business days, with a response target of 2 business days.
An escalation score is a recommendation for human triage and never automatically issues a refund, cancels an order, or promises a resolution time.
