# Warranty terms by category

Electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty shown on the product page, and a warranty claim requires the invoice and serial number.
Apparel and Footwear have no extended warranty in this demonstration policy; manufacturing defects must be reported within their return window.
Home items have a manufacturer warranty only when the product listing explicitly offers one.
Beauty items have no usage warranty, and adverse reactions should be referred to a qualified medical professional without a diagnosis from support.
