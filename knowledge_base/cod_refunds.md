# Cash on delivery refund timelines

For a cash on delivery (COD) order, a refund is initiated after the returned item passes the warehouse quality check.
COD refunds reach the customer's verified bank account within 5 to 7 business days after refund initiation.
The customer must submit bank details through the secure account form, and support must never request an OTP or a complete card number in chat.
If the refund is missing after 7 business days, support creates a payments escalation using the return reference.
