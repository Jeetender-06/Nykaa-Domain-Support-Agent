# Delivery service levels

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days for metro destinations and 5 to 8 business days for other serviceable Indian PIN codes, measured from dispatch.
The estimated delivery date shown at checkout is the reference for a shipment-delay inquiry.
A shipment that has not moved for 48 hours after the estimated delivery date should be escalated to the logistics team.
Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays, and support cannot promise an exact delivery hour.
