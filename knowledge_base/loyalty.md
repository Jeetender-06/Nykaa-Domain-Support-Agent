# Loyalty points redemption

Loyalty points can be redeemed at checkout once the account has at least 100 available points.
Each loyalty point is worth INR 1, and points may cover at most 20 percent of the eligible product subtotal.
Points cannot pay shipping fees, buy gift cards, or be exchanged for cash.
Redeemed points from a fully cancelled order return to the same account within 48 hours after cancellation confirmation.
