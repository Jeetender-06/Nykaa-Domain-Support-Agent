# Order cancellation policy

An order can be cancelled from the account page while its status is Placed and before warehouse packing begins.
Once packing or shipping starts, cancellation is unavailable and the customer must use the applicable return process after delivery.
A prepaid cancellation refund is initiated to the original payment method within 2 business days of cancellation confirmation.
Support can explain cancellation eligibility but cannot cancel or modify an order through this chat.
